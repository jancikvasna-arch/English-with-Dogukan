Photo treatment options

The photo is already a good quality headshot. These options show how CSS filters change the mood without any photo editing software — and how cropping changes the formality level.

Original
Clean B&W. Professional, formal. Studio feel.
no filter
Warm Sepia
Adds warmth and approachability. Less "LinkedIn", more personal.
sepia(0.25) brightness(1.06)
Soft B&W
Slightly softer, less harsh contrast. Friendlier tone.
brightness(1.08) contrast(0.88)
Cropped + Warm
Tighter crop removes most of the suit — face-forward feels more casual and approachable.
crop + sepia(0.25)
💡 To make the photo feel more casual
  • The suit and tie are the main source of formality — a tighter crop that shows face and collar only softens this significantly without any retouching
  • The warm sepia filter (option 2) is the single best CSS change — it transforms the clinical B&W into something warmer and more personal
  • The grey background in the photo is neutral and works with all three colour concepts — no need to remove it
  • If Dogukan has a more casual photo (outdoors, lighter clothing, natural light) that would be ideal for a tutoring site — but this one works well with the treatments above
  • We can use the cropped + warm version in the hero and the original in the About Me section for a natural contrast
